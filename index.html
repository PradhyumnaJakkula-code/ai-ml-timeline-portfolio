Artifact Details

1. Title

AI and ML Timelines: Tracing the Evolution of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Technologies

2. Introduction

As a Java developer transitioning into AI and Data Analytics, understanding the historical evolution of AI and ML is essential to my professional growth. This timeline artifact serves as both a learning tool and a resource that demonstrates how foundational concepts built the modern AI landscape I'm now entering.

3. Description

An interactive visual timeline (created in Canva) that traces key milestones in AI and ML development—from the Dartmouth Conference (1956) through contemporary deep learning advances. The timeline includes:

  • Pivotal moments in AI history (Turing Test, Expert Systems, Neural Networks, Deep Learning)
  • Key breakthroughs by decade
  • Influential researchers and their contributions
  • Transition points from symbolic AI to data-driven approaches
  • Current state and emerging trends (LLMs, Transformers, Federated Learning)
4. Objective
  • To consolidate my foundational knowledge of how AI/ML evolved.
  • To create a visual reference guide I can use when discussing AI evolution with peers and potential employers.
  • To demonstrate my ability to synthesize complex historical information into an accessible format.
  • To show my appreciation for how traditional Java development is now intersecting with AI technologies.
5. Process
  • Researched major milestones in AI/ML history from course materials and credible sources.
  • Identified 15–20 key events spanning 1956 to 2026.
  • Organized chronologically and thematically (pure AI → ML → Deep Learning → Current Trends).
  • Used Canva's timeline template to design a clean, professional visualization.
  • Added brief descriptions, dates, and relevance for each milestone.
  • Color-coded by era/category for quick visual reference.
6. Tools and Technologies Used
  • Canva (primary design tool)
  • Course materials on ML fundamentals
  • Research from AI history sources
7. Value Proposition

For Employers

My ability to contextualize modern AI within its historical development—showing I'm not just learning tools, but understanding the why behind current approaches.

For Peers/Collaborators

A shareable reference guide that makes AI history accessible and prevents reinventing the wheel.

For Myself

An anchoring artifact that bridges my Java development background with my new AI/ML focus, showing how the field has evolved to meet modern computational challenges.

8. Unique Value

Unlike generic AI timelines found online, this artifact is:

  • Tailored to the IWU ML course curriculum: emphasizing concepts I'm actively learning.
  • Designed as a professional resource: not just a homework submission.
  • Positioned at the intersection of traditional software development and modern AI: reflecting my career transition.
